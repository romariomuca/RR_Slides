Overview

The rules behind the AppV5 prototype: tone, colour, type, shape and states.

Riding the Rainbow (Solidarity Hub) is Afrilanthropy's neighbourhood app in Luxembourg: people ask for and offer items, activities and events, talk in chat and meet in safe places. The interface is light and calm, in the spirit of Airbnb and Vinted: lots of white, one ink colour, and six rainbow colours used only to carry meaning.

This system describes the AppV5 prototype after the alignment of 19 November 2026: the app's colours, CTAs, labels and copy already use these tokens. The Status and next steps section lists what has been unified and what is still open (radii, shadows, section titles).

Content and tone

  • The UI is in English, in sentence case: "Mark as received", "Your post is live". Never Title Case on buttons.
  • Address people as "you", direct and warm, with no exclamation marks and no emoji in the UI.
  • Every thing has one name: Chat (bar and page title), Items · Activities · Events (always plural in tabs, Post included), Needed · Offered · Event for the post type, Rainbow points for points.
  • Errors say what happened and what to do: "Not sent · Tap to retry", "Location is off · Showing Luxembourg City centre".
  • Separate metadata with a middle dot: ~700 m · Bonnevoie · 2 h ago.

Colour

  • The base is neutral. The page background is surface, cards and sheets are surface-card, secondary elements use fill, borders use line.
  • Text has three levels: ink for primary, ink-2 for secondary, ink-3 for metadata, labels and inactive items. Lighter greys (#A3A39E) are only for decoration such as street names on the map, never for text people need to read.
  • Inactive filter chips are surface-card with shadow-sm on every screen (Home, Map, Chat). The active one is ink. fill is for secondary elements, not for filter chips.
  • The only solid action on a screen is ink with on-ink text. Black is the primary colour, not the rainbow.
  • The rainbow (coral, orange, yellow, mint, blue, purple) codes categories and types: Needed coral, Offered mint, Event purple, Items orange. Use it on rings, icons, pins and tints (*-soft), never as small text colour: orange, yellow and mint are below 2.5:1 on white.
  • The rainbow gradient belongs to the brand: Rainbow points and the invite QR frame. Gradient text only at 20px and above.
  • The Home and Post headers have a soft gradient in the type's colour that fades into surface. Map, Chat, Notifications and the Profile pages have a neutral header.
  • Semantic colours are separate from the rainbow:
  • Safe places and verification: safe for pins and badges, safe-ink for text, safe-soft for backgrounds.
  • Errors and destructive actions: danger, on surface-card only.
  • Urgency: urgent-ink on danger-soft.
  • Expired: warning-ink on yellow-soft.
  • The unread dot and badge are coral with on-ink text.

Typography

  • One family: Poppins (Google Fonts), weights 400 and 500. 600 is not used.
  • page-title for tabs (Chat, Profile), display for onboarding and confirmation overlays, section-title for sheets and large sections, card-title for card titles.
  • Running text is body. List rows and descriptions are body-sm, metadata is meta in ink-3.
  • Section labels are label (11px, uppercase, 1.5px tracking, ink-3).
  • Controls use button for CTAs, chip for chips and filters, tag for status pills.
  • Headings get negative tracking (−0.02 to −0.03em); running text gets none.

Space, shape, depth

  • The side gutter is space-16. Cards sit space-12 apart, chips space-8, sections space-24.
  • Radii:
  • radius-full for everything you tap: buttons, chips, pills, avatars, bottom bar.
  • radius-lg for list cards.
  • radius-xl for hero cards and sheets.
  • radius-md for photos and bubbles.
  • radius-sm for thumbnails.
  • Shadows:
  • shadow-card for cards on surface.
  • shadow-float for anything floating (bottom bar, toasts, banners, sheets).
  • shadow-sm for filter chips and pins.
  • No coloured borders on cards.
  • Control heights: control-lg (52px) for the primary CTA and inputs, control-md (38px) for secondary buttons, control-chip (36px) for filter chips, control-sm (32px) for quick replies and small buttons, icon-btn (44px) for round buttons.
  • A pill inside a pill is concentric: inner radius = outer radius − padding (bottom bar 33px, active item 27px with 6px padding).

Iconography

  • Icons are Lucide, 1.6–1.8px stroke, in ink (or on-ink on black). Sizes: 16, 19 and 22px.
  • Category icons sit in a radius-sm square with a *-soft background and the glyph in the full colour.
  • No emoji or text symbols (✓, ★) in the UI: use the matching Lucide icon.
  • No logo or mark is in the sources: set the name in Poppins 500.

States

  • Offline: an ink pill with on-ink text at the top: "You're offline · we'll send things when you're back".
  • Failed send: "Not sent · Tap to retry" in danger under the bubble.
  • Permission denied: a surface-card banner with shadow-float, an icon, two lines and an ink "Turn on" CTA.
  • Loading: skeletons in fill with a shimmer. Empty: a title, one line and a single CTA.
  • Toast: an ink pill that stays 2 seconds, with a ✕ to dismiss it.

Components

Live previews built from the tokens, with usage rules.

Actions

Button

The primary CTA is solid black: one per screen, for the main action ("Continue", "Send request", "Publish").

  • Primary: ink + on-ink, control-lg high (52px), radius-full, button text. Full width at the bottom of sheets and flows.
  • Secondary: surface-card with a 1px line-strong border ("Save draft", "Open chat").
  • Medium: control-md (38px), 13px text, for actions inside cards and banners ("Turn on", "Mark as received").
  • Destructive: danger text with no fill. Destructive confirmations live in a sheet.
  • Icon only: an icon-btn circle (44px), surface-card with shadow-sm (back, filters, close).

Labels are a verb in sentence case. Never use the rainbow on buttons.

Actions

Chip

Chips filter content and switch views: the Chat tags, the Map suggestions, the categories.

  • Chip: control-chip high (36px), radius-full, chip text. Inactive: surface-card with shadow-sm, the same in Home, Map and Chat. Active: ink + on-ink. Never a grey fill background.
  • Counter: a coral pill with on-ink text (unread, requests). On an active chip it turns white.
  • Segmented: a fill track with the selected segment in surface-card and shadow-sm (Calendar | List, Week | Month).

Chips sit in a horizontally scrolling row with a space-8 gap.

Navigation

BottomBar

The main navigation: a floating pill with five items, Home, Map, Post, Chat, Profile.

  • surface-card, 66px high, 33px radius, shadow-float, lifted off the bottom edge. Behind it sits a 72px fade into surface, so content doesn't scroll visibly behind the bar.
  • Items are 54px high with a 27px radius, concentric with the bar (6px padding). The active item has a fill background and ink text; the others are ink-3. Labels are 10px/500.
  • Post is an ink circle with a "+" in on-ink.
  • Chat carries the unread badge: a coral pill, on-ink text, a 2px surface-card border. It counts unread conversations.
Content

PostCard

The list card in Home: photo, type, title, distance and author.

  • surface-card, radius-lg, shadow-card, space-12 padding.
  • The photo is 88px with radius-md. Without a photo: the category tint as background with the icon in the full colour.
  • In order: the type tag (plus "Today" when urgent), the card-title title (15–17px), meta in ink-3 as ~distance · neighbourhood · time, then the author with a 20px avatar.
  • Cards stack in a column with a space-12 gap. Tapping a card opens the post detail.
Content

CategoryIcon

The coloured square that identifies a category or type, in cards, filters and map pins.

  • 44px, radius-sm, a *-soft tint background, a 22px Lucide icon in the full colour.
  • Colours follow the code: Needed coral, Items orange, Activities mint, Events purple. Categories reuse the six colours; "Other" is ink on fill.
  • On the map the same colour becomes the 1.5px ring around the white pin. Safe places are safe.
Status

StatusTag

Status pills inside cards and rows: they show the post type, urgency or a listing's state.

  • 22px high, radius-full, tag text (10px/500).
  • Post type: coral-soft (Needed), mint-soft (Offered) or purple-soft (Event) background with ink text. The full rainbow colour is never used as text.
  • Urgency: urgent-ink on danger-soft. One style for both "Today" and "Urgent".
  • My posts: Live is safe-ink on mint-soft, Expired is warning-ink on yellow-soft, Given is ink-2 on fill.
  • Safety: "Safe meeting place" in safe-ink on safe-soft.
Forms

TextField

Form fields (Post, Edit profile, search).

  • A label above the field, with a danger asterisk when the field is required.
  • The field is control-lg high (52px), radius-md, surface-card with a 1px line-strong border, body text. The placeholder is ink-3 and gives a concrete example.
  • Focus is a 2px ink ring. The hint below is meta in ink-3.
  • The Home search uses the same field with radius-full and a search icon.
Feedback

Toast

Short confirmations and the offline state.

  • Toast: an ink pill with on-ink text (13px/500) and shadow-float, at the bottom above the bottom bar. It stays 2 seconds and has a ✕ to dismiss it. The text says what happened, in the past tense: "Draft saved", "Link copied".
  • Offline: the same pill, shorter (34px), fixed at the top of the screen until the connection is back.
Feedback

Banner

Error states that don't block the screen: permission denied and failed send.

  • Permission denied: a surface-card card, radius-lg, shadow-float. It holds an icon, a title (14px/500) saying what is missing, one ink-3 line saying what we show instead, and a medium ink CTA to fix it ("Turn on").
  • Failed send: under the bubble, "Not sent · Tap to retry" in danger at 12px. Tapping retries.

Never colour alone: there is always text that explains.

Status and next steps

Where the alignment between the AppV5 prototype and this system stands.

Already aligned in the app

  • Text: only ink, ink-2 and ink-3. The too-light grey (#8A8A86) is gone from text.
  • Surfaces: only surface, surface-card, fill, line and line-strong, instead of seven near-identical light greys.
  • Destructive actions and errors: one red, danger, for Delete, Report, Block, Discard, "Not sent" and asterisks.
  • Safety and confirmations: one green for text, safe-ink, plus safe for pins and badges.
  • Urgency: "Today" and "Urgent" share one style, urgent-ink on danger-soft.
  • Primary CTA: always control-lg, 52px.
  • Uppercase labels: one variant, label.
  • Font weights: 400 and 500 only.
  • Filter chips: white with shadow-sm in Home, Map and Chat.
  • Bottom bar: concentric pills, plus a fade under the bar so content doesn't scroll visibly behind it.
  • Copy: "Events" everywhere, "Offered" in post rows, "Done" without a symbol. The "Under review" status has been removed.

Still to do

  • Radii: the app still uses 31 different values. Map them to the 5 tokens: list cards to radius-lg, hero cards and sheets to radius-xl, photos to radius-md.
  • Shadows: 57 variants. Reduce them to shadow-sm, shadow-card and shadow-float.
  • Section titles: 25, 26, 28 and 30px. Unify on section-title (26px), with display (34px) for onboarding and confirmations.
  • Colours outside the system: about 80 hex values remain, used only in illustrations, header gradients, the map and generated avatars. They are decorative and can stay outside the tokens, as long as they are never used for text or controls.
