Where we come from

Born in 2022, when refugees from Ukraine arrived in Luxembourg: children and families looking for a new beginning and a welcoming community.

So we created an app that connects people who donate pre-loved items with those who need them.

Our original logoRiding the Rainbow, since 2022

New Item donation

Donors post pre-loved items with a photo, the age they fit and their condition. Families ask for what they need and arrange the handover.

Photo and conditionRequest in one tapVolunteer delivery

People can say what they need, starting with items. A weekly limit keeps it fair, and every post is moderated before it goes live.
