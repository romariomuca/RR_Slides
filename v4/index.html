Project overview
01

Project summary

SolidarityHub connects people in need with people and organisations willing to support them. A responsible, human-centred app that turns acts of kindness, like donating pre-loved items, into collective change and real human connection.

For families
Refugee families, often with children, get items and support they need.
For donors
Neighbours give what they no longer use, and meet the people they help.
For NGOs
Partner organisations reach more people and post their events.

05

What technology are you building and how does it address this cohort's theme?

A community platform that connects vulnerable people with local support. We use open-weight models with privacy and safety safeguards, not frontier models.

Translation
Built into the chat, so donors and families who share no language can talk.
Help assistant
Answers only from the app's own data: available items, events and how the app works.
Harmful content detection
Keeps the platform safe for vulnerable users.
Project details
06

Small: what models, hardware and infrastructure does your project use, and why are they the right fit?

Models
Gemma 4 31B for reasoning and tool use. Clef 27B and NeMo Guardrails check every input and output.
Orchestration
LangChain and LangGraph: the model only acts through app tools (find items, explain the app, find volunteering) or asks the user to clarify.
Infrastructure
Hosted inference on Melious.ai with zero retention. Google Translate API for chat translation today.
Next step
Move narrow tasks such as translation and classification to smaller, self-hosted models.
Project details
07

Intentional: how do users understand and control how their data is collected, stored and used?

Minimal data
Only name, email and, in some cases, location: what we need to match help.
Zero retention
Prompts sent to Melious.ai are not stored. Messages are translated only on request, without identifying the user.
Today
Conversations are saved in the app and visible only to the user.
With the grant
Zero retention on the app side too, and open-source code communities can run themselves.

16

What novel approaches to building AI systems does your work introduce to the field?

Tool-bound, not generative
The model can only act on verified app data, so it cannot invent help that doesn't exist.
Dignity-first guardrails
Never asks for proof of need; sensitive situations go to a human.
Task-sized models
Small specialised models instead of one big assistant.
Configurable by NGOs
Organisations set languages, services and rules without code.
