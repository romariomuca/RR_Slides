01

Where we come from

Born in 2022 as a response to the inflow of refugees from Ukraine and other countries. Bike distribution gave a new beginning, revealed the willingness of people to do more and created connections between people.

So we created an app that connects people who donate pre-loved items with those who need them.

Our original logoRiding the Rainbow, since 2022
